Verification & state integrity

Every claim is verifiable before it ships.

State integrity is the property that makes Spock's output usable in the partner room. Every claim that reaches the IC paper carries its source, its tier, and the verdict of an independent verifier. The integrity is a system property — not an after-the-fact review.

Three mechanisms compose to produce it.

01

Paired verifiers

Every agent is paired with an independent fact-checker. The verifier reads the same source documents directly and tries to break the agent's claims. No claim ships to the IC paper without a paired verdict. Verifier and agent are different model contexts, with different prompts, scoped to the same inputs.

02

Information sanctity tiers

Every claim travels with the tier of its source. T1 primary: audited statements, court records, commissioned diligence. T2 named-byline secondary: FT, Reuters, Business Daily. T3 aggregators: industry blogs — triangulation only. T4 unverified: Glassdoor, Reddit, anonymous reviews — flagged, never load-bearing.

03

Audit trail

Source citation, tier, and verifier verdict travel attached to each claim through every downstream pipeline stage. Consolidation, scenario analysis, sensitivity table, IC paper section: every output traces back to a primary source. No copy-paste. No silent rewrites.

Verification in practice

The mechanism only matters if it catches things. Two examples from the Pegasus run.

The system is designed assuming agents make mistakes — frontier models confabulate, read charts wrong, miss sub-lines in long tables. The point of verification is that mistakes never reach the IC paper, not that they never happen at all.

Example 01
$253m → $418m

A revenue table read incomplete.

Agent output
$253m FY24 revenue.
Verifier action
Opened the Companies House audited Strategic Report KPI table directly. Re-read every revenue sub-line.
Verifier finding
Total revenue $418m — the $164.5m interest income line had been missed. The interest line is now 40% of revenue and the visible signature of the AI underwriting flywheel.
Outcome
Corrected baseline of $418m carried into every downstream output. EY FDD cross-checked at $419.5m total revenue — $1.5m of agreement.
Example 02
$546m → $775m

A DCF starting input from a superseded projection.

Agent output
DCF model initialised with $546m FY26 revenue.
Verifier action
Traced the input back to its source document. Identified $546m as a Sep 2024 management projection now superseded by the May 2026 Long Range Plan.
Verifier finding
Current LRP figure for FY26 revenue is $775m. The Sep 2024 number is no longer the company's own view.
Outcome
Re-run with the corrected starting point gives Chronos equity value of $1.05bn against Round F sponsor anchor of $699m. +49% intrinsic-value premium to entry vs +23% on the uncorrected model.
Why this exists

A 500-page substrate has thousands of claims. Frontier-model agents read at human speed and produce structured output — but they are not infallible readers. The cost of an unchecked claim reaching the IC paper is measured in lost capital and lost partner attention. Verification is built in by design because the alternative — partner-room corrections in a 90-minute meeting — does not scale.

What it does not do
  • — Replace partner judgement on the substantive thesis
  • — Adjudicate between two well-sourced but disagreeing views
  • — Verify claims that cannot be reduced to a primary source
  • — Make claims based on management meeting notes load-bearing
