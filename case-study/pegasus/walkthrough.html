The Pegasus Run

We ran Spock v4 on the Pegasus deal.

Standard Bank invited Chronos into a $33.9m syndicate at Round F (October 2025). Chronos slot: $13.9m / c. 1.99% of Pegasus.

The substrate: four PDFs — Management Presentation (Sep 2024), FTI Commercial Due Diligence (Sep 2024), EY Financial Due Diligence (July 2025), Long Range Plan (May 2026).

Plus four months of regulatory and legal events — Kenya Tax Tribunal rulings, High Court filings, a Capital Markets Authority complaint.

Catch #1 · The $165m Revenue Error
$253m → $418m

The Company History agent said Pegasus's FY24 revenue was $253m.

The fact-checker opened the audited Companies House accounts directly. Actual figure: $418m total. The agent had read only the goods-sale sub-line and missed the $164.5m interest income line.

$165m material error caught — before it propagated into the IC paper.

Source: Companies House page 4 directors' Strategic Report KPI table. Cross-checked at EY FDD page 31 ($419.5m total revenue, $1.5m apart from Companies House).

Catch #2 · Confabulation
Verified vs Inferred

The Hardware Moat agent reported: "Samsung Knox Guard — Pegasus's lockout technology — is also used by Watu Credit. The moat is eroding."

The fact-checker flagged: the word "Knox" appears nowhere in Pegasus's substrate. Whether Pegasus actually uses Knox Guard is analyst inference, not verified fact.

Confabulation caught. The Knox question is now an IC-blocking question for the Nairobi management session.

FTI Commercial DD page 40 says Pegasus's tech stack was "developed in collaboration with Pegasus" — inconsistent with off-the-shelf Knox Guard.

Catch #3 · Chart Read Backwards
Former 4.3% · New AI 2.2%

The AI Unit Economics agent reported the New AI Model was at 4.3% prediction-error and the Former Model was at 2.2% — i.e. AI was worse than the rules-based predecessor.

The fact-checker opened management's slide deck and re-read the chart directly. The values were flipped. Former Model is 4.3%. New AI Model is 2.2%. The AI is roughly half the error rate.

Chart-read error caught. The AI thesis pillar is now confirmed at "grade B" — real, with a defined path to "grade A" via cohort decomposition evidence.
Catch #4 · The Stale Forecast Input
$546m → $775m

The DCF financial model was built using $546m as Pegasus's FY26 starting revenue.

The fact-checker traced this back. $546m was an older Sep 2024 projection — now superseded. Pegasus's own Long Range Plan FY26 figure is $775m.

Re-running the DCF with the corrected starting point gives Chronos equity value of $1.05 billion versus the Round F sponsor anchor of $699m.

+49% intrinsic-value premium to entry, not the +23% the uncorrected model showed. Material for the Standard Bank conversation.
Information Sanctity · Every Claim Travels With a Tier

Not every source is equal. Spock keeps track.

T1 · Primary

Audited statements. Court records. Patent registries. Companies House filings. Independently-commissioned diligence reports.

T2 · Named-byline Secondary

Financial Times. Reuters. Business Daily Africa. Named-byline reporting.

T3 · Aggregators

Industry blogs and aggregator sites. TechCabal. TechCrunch. Useful for triangulation, not for headline claims.

T4 · Unverified

Glassdoor. Reddit. Anonymous reviews. Flagged but never load-bearing.

Tiers stay attached as claims move through the pipeline. No T4 claim ever ships as load-bearing.

Standing Rules · Chronos Discipline Built In

Four rules apply to every IC paper Spock produces.

Rule 1 · No Cumulative Vanity Metrics

Commercial sections use active figures. No "X million customers since inception." Forces analysts to talk about what's working now, not what's accumulated.

Rule 2 · Indexing Convention

100 = peer median, always. All comparative scoring lives in the 50–150 range. No 1–5 scales. No 0–100 ladders. The convention forces every score to be referenced against something real.

Rule 3 · Chronos Scar Tissue Stays Internal

If Chronos has historical experience with a sector or jurisdiction that informs risk weighting — that experience shapes the analysis but is never named in the paper body. We don't burn portfolio relationships.

Rule 4 · Directional Inputs Are Never Evidence

Meeting notes. Verbal-only diligence inputs. Recorder transcripts. These shape questions; they never cite as evidence. If a meeting note disagrees with audited data — the meeting becomes a management question, not a paper claim.
