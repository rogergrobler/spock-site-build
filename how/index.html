The four discipline pillars

What makes Spock's output partner-grade isn't the AI. It's the discipline that runs around the AI.

Four invariants apply to every claim Spock produces — across both pipelines, across every deal type.

1

Paired verifiers on every agent

Every agent ships its claims paired with a verifier verdict. The verifier reads the same source documents independently, with a different prompt, and tries to break the agent's claims. No claim reaches the IC paper without a paired verdict. State integrity is a system property — covered in detail on Verification.

2

Information sanctity tiers

Every claim travels with the tier of its source. T1 primary: audited statements, court records, commissioned diligence. T2 named-byline secondary: FT, Reuters, Business Daily. T3 aggregators: industry blogs, useful for triangulation only. T4 unverified: Glassdoor, Reddit, anonymous reviews — flagged, never load-bearing.

3

A doctrine library that travels with the deal

Spock pulls from a doctrine library — pattern catalogues, sector threat lists, gate-criteria — built up across every Chronos deal. The library is what gives Spock a view, not just a method. Examples: the fintech-receivables risk pattern catalogue (28 named patterns), the AI-Tool-User gate criteria, the standing-rules document.

4

Standing rules that pre-empt mistakes

Rule 1. No cumulative vanity metrics in commercial sections. Rule 2. Indexing convention: 100 = peer median, range ~50–150. No 1–5 ladders, no 0–100 scales. Rule 3. Chronos scar tissue stays internal — never named in the paper body. Rule 4. Directional inputs are never evidence — meeting notes shape questions, not claims.
